1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile games published by Continue? (Continue Questionmark Studio), an independent game studio run by Paco Camarena, based in Valencia, Spain (the “Studio”, “we”, “us”). It covers all our games, including PopPerfect (Android, package com.continuequestionmark.popperfect), and any future game that links to this page.

We are the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Spanish data protection law (LOPDGDD). Contact: continue.questionmark.studio@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

Our games do not require an account or login, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number or any other directly identifying information. We do not sell personal data.

However, our games include third-party software development kits (SDKs) used to show advertising and to keep the game working. These SDKs may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • Device information (model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network/carrier type);
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it;
  • App usage and ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, installs, session information);
  • Crash, performance and diagnostic data.

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the content of your message, which we use only to answer you.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Showing personalised ads – based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), where required by law.
  • Showing non-personalised ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and ad measurement – based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in funding free games, or on consent where the law requires it.
  • Keeping the game working, fixing crashes and improving performance – legitimate interest.
  • Answering your messages – legitimate interest / taking steps at your request.
  • Complying with legal obligations – legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

5. Consent and how to opt out

  • Where required (for example in the EEA, UK or Switzerland), our games may show a consent message before personalised ads are served. You can refuse, and you can change your choice later from the in-game privacy option, if available, or by contacting us.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).
  • You can also uninstall the game at any time to stop all further collection by the game.

If you opt out, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

6. Children

Our games are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, 14 in Spain). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

We do not store gameplay data on our own servers. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them. Data collected by third-party SDKs is kept according to those providers’ retention policies.

8. International transfers

Some providers (such as Google and Unity) may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Such transfers rely on safeguards recognised by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting prior processing). To exercise them, email continue.questionmark.studio@gmail.com. Because we do not hold identifying data ourselves, we may refer you to the relevant provider or ask for your advertising ID to help locate data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos – AEPD) or with the supervisory authority of your country.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information we handle and we choose providers that apply appropriate security measures. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we release a new game or add a provider. The updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be notified in-game where appropriate.

5. Consentimiento y cómo oponerte

  • Cuando sea obligatorio (por ejemplo, en el EEE, Reino Unido o Suiza), el juego puede mostrar un mensaje de consentimiento antes de servir anuncios personalizados. Puedes rechazarlo y cambiar tu elección más tarde desde la opción de privacidad del juego, si está disponible, o escribiéndonos.
  • En Android puedes restablecer o eliminar tu ID de publicidad, o desactivar los anuncios personalizados, en Ajustes › Google › Anuncios (o Ajustes › Privacidad › Anuncios, según el dispositivo).
  • También puedes desinstalar el juego en cualquier momento para detener toda recogida de datos por su parte.

Si te opones, seguirás viendo anuncios, pero serán menos relevantes para ti.

6. Menores

Nuestros juegos están pensados para un público general y no están dirigidos a menores de 13 años (ni de la edad mínima de consentimiento digital en tu país, 14 años en España). No recogemos conscientemente datos personales de menores. Si crees que un menor nos ha facilitado datos, contáctanos y los eliminaremos.

7. Conservación de los datos

No almacenamos datos de juego en servidores propios. Los correos que nos envíes se conservan solo el tiempo necesario para atender tu solicitud y después se eliminan, salvo obligación legal. Los datos recogidos por los SDK de terceros se conservan según las políticas de esos proveedores.

Algunos proveedores (como Google y Unity) pueden tratar datos fuera del Espacio Económico Europeo, incluido Estados Unidos. Estas transferencias se basan en garantías reconocidas por el RGPD, como el Marco de Privacidad de Datos UE-EE. UU. cuando el proveedor está certificado, o las Cláusulas Contractuales Tipo de la Comisión Europea.

9. Tus derechos

Según el RGPD tienes derecho de acceso, rectificación, supresión, limitación y oposición al tratamiento, derecho a la portabilidad y derecho a retirar tu consentimiento en cualquier momento (sin afectar al tratamiento previo). Para ejercerlos, escribe a continue.questionmark.studio@gmail.com. Como no guardamos datos identificativos, podemos remitirte al proveedor correspondiente o pedirte tu ID de publicidad para localizar los datos.

También puedes presentar una reclamación ante la Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD) o ante la autoridad de control de tu país.

10. Seguridad

Aplicamos medidas razonables para proteger la información que tratamos y elegimos proveedores con medidas de seguridad adecuadas. Aun así, ningún método de transmisión o almacenamiento es totalmente seguro.
